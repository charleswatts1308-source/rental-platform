# What happens after you raise a repair case

A plain-English walkthrough of how renters.rent handles a repair case
from start to finish. Written for tenants (and anyone who wants the
non-technical picture).

Default timings are shown below. These can be changed in settings — the
family trial runs on shortened intervals so the whole journey is visible
in days rather than weeks.

---

## 1. You open the case

You write up the repair issue and we show you exactly what your landlord
will receive. Once you confirm, **the first notice goes to your landlord
straight away**, signed in your name "via renters.rent." Their reply
comes back to the case — not to your personal inbox.

## 2. We wait for your landlord

Your landlord has **14 days** to respond. Two things can happen:

- **They reply** → the reply lands on your case, we email you to let you
  know, and the clock resets. The ball's now with you to decide the next
  step (reply, pause, or close).
- **They stay silent** → after 14 days, the next notice goes out
  automatically. We email you each time to say "we've sent notice 2 / 3
  / 4 to your landlord."

## 3. If silence continues

We send up to **4 notices in total**, each 14 days apart. If your
landlord ignores the whole sequence, we send a **final closing letter**
— a firmer letter noting the matter may now go to environmental health,
an ombudsman, or court — and we let you know the escalation process has
run its course. Your full correspondence record is then ready to use as
evidence. **We don't act for you with those bodies — the next move is
yours.**

## 4. Special case: your landlord replied once, then went quiet

If your landlord *engaged* at some point and then stopped, **we don't
fire the next notice automatically.** Instead we email you: *"Your
landlord hasn't replied — ready to send the next notice?"* with a link
to review and send it yourself. You stay in control of escalating
against someone who's shown they're reachable.

## 5. If the case is waiting on *you*

Sometimes the ball is in your court (e.g. your landlord asked a
question). If you go quiet:

- After **10 days** we send a gentle nudge.
- After **20 days**, one more nudge.
- After **30 days** with no response, the case goes **dormant** —
  nothing is lost; a reply from you at any time picks it straight back
  up.

## 6. If you've paused the case

If you put the case on hold (say your landlord gave a fix date), it sits
paused until the hold ends — then it resumes and the landlord clock
starts again, and we let you know.

---

## Two things stay true throughout

- **You're always told** when a letter goes out, and you can pause or
  close the case at any time.
- **Your contact details stay private** — landlords reply through us,
  never directly to you, to protect against retaliation.
