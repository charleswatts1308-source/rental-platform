Aggregate reporting (rua) is retained, to both Mailgun and OnDMARC.

NOTES
================================================================

Why the apex needs its own records at all
-----------------------------------------
Settled by the mail identity audit, 2026-08-01. The apex is used as a
sending identity in one place: app/Mail/ContactReply.php hardcodes
from: 'noreply@renters.rent'. Everything else sends from the mail
subdomain (case notices from cases@mg.renters.rent) or from whatever
MAIL_FROM_ADDRESS resolves to per environment.

So the apex records cover ContactReply, and any auth mail if
MAIL_FROM_ADDRESS on production turns out to be an apex address —
CONFIRM with: php artisan config:show mail.from   (on the box; use
config:show rather than reading .env, per the duplicate-key trap).

Both new policies are p=none — monitoring only, nothing is rejected or
quarantined. This is a baseline to observe from, not enforcement.

RFC reference
-------------
The deprecation of pct and ri was cited as RFC 9989 at the time of
writing. Worth verifying the number before quoting it anywhere external
— DMARCbis went through several draft numbers. The change itself stands
on its own merits regardless of the citation.

inbox.renters.rent — does not exist
-----------------------------------
Flagged during the same audit. docs/deploy-checklist.md lines 24 and 60
give inbox.renters.rent as the production MAILGUN_INBOUND_DOMAIN,
including in a copy-paste .env block. That domain has no MX and no A
record. Production actually uses mg.renters.rent. Following that
checklist on a rebuild would silently break inbound landlord replies.
The checklist has NOT been corrected yet.
