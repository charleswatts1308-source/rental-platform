--- #30 Contact Us — MODEL CHOSEN ---------------------------------

Charlie's model, agreed: posts and replies recorded in a table, with
the respective participant notified by email when a new post goes in.

Consequence that CHANGES the earlier assessment: email is a
NOTIFICATION, not the transport. Nothing is received, so this is
outbound only, so it CAN be tested on staging. The earlier warning
that a threaded Contact Us must be proven on production applied to an
inbound-email design and does NOT apply to this one.

Today's table is one row per enquiry: subject, message, a single
admin_reply, replied_at. One slot for one answer, which is why a
second exchange has nowhere to go. The model turns that row into a
conversation header with posts in a second table, author and body per
row; the existing message and reply migrate in as the first two posts.

Contact Us sits behind ['auth','verified'], so every participant has
an account and can be linked to the thread. That removes the hardest
problem in the design — how an anonymous enquirer would ever see a
reply.

Build note: a new table means a manual MariaDB check before merge, per
CLAUDE.md. SQLite cannot show the ON UPDATE CURRENT_TIMESTAMP trap.

--- #48 admin address — APPROACH AGREED ---------------------------

Use admin@mg.renters.rent with a Mailgun FORWARD ROUTE to Charlie's
private address, collected in Outlook. Same pattern as the Plesk
forward used on the other site. NO DNS change, NO hosting change, NO
second mail system, no mailbox to maintain.

Why this works: mg.renters.rent already receives through Mailgun. The
apex renters.rent has no MX at all, which is why mail to
admin@renters.rent hits the web server and vanishes.

CONDITION — route ordering. Case reply addresses are
{token}@mg.renters.rent. If the existing Mailgun route matches
everything arriving at that subdomain, it will swallow admin@ too,
hand it to the inbound controller, match no token, and treat it as an
unmatched message. The admin@ route must sit AHEAD of the case-reply
route on priority.
TO CHECK in the Mailgun dashboard: is the existing route a catch-all,
or does it only match token-shaped addresses?

Steps, in order:
  1. Mailgun route: recipient admin@mg.renters.rent, forward to the
     private address, priority above the case-reply route.
  2. One row on production: change the admin account's login email to
     that address. Password reset then works.
  3. Two published pages repointed: privacy.blade.php:60 and
     cookies.blade.php:85 name the old address as the data-protection
     contact.
  4. Two dev seeders: DevReset.php:56 and DevLifecycle.php:29 create
     the admin with the old address.
  5. LAST, and only after the new address is proven: clear the
     Mailgun suppression entry for admin@renters.rent. Clearing it
     first simply re-suppresses on the next send.
Test: send from an outside account and confirm it lands in Outlook,
then run a password reset on the admin account and confirm the link
arrives.

Accepted cost: the published compliance address will read with "mg."
in the middle. A working address that looks odd beats a tidy one that
discards mail. It can move later without changing anything else.

--- LANDLORD WRITTEN CHANNEL — DECIDED, minimal version ------------

No public form, no landlord accounts, nothing else built.

Target is /landlords, NOT Contact Us. Contact Us sits behind
['auth','verified'] and registration is allowlisted during beta, so a
landlord clicking it hits a login wall with no way through — the
#46/#49/#53 pattern again, a surface promising what the system will
not honour. /landlords is public, was built for this audience, and

address too, so the link delivers a written channel with no new page.

database/seeders/LetterTemplateSeeder.php and appears in at least TWO
variants (around lines 229 and 276) — one mentions routed replies, the

link is then one pass over the seeder rather than two.

Wording must keep the two routes distinct: replying to the email is
about the REPAIR and lands in the case record; the link is for
everything else. If both read the same, landlords use whichever comes
first.

NOT built, considered and rejected for now:
  - a public landlord contact form (one route outside the auth group,
    a controller method, a view, a nullable user column, honeypot and
    rate limit). Best value if the link proves insufficient.
  - landlord accounts. Far bigger than the problem.

THE OPEN QUESTION CHARLIE IS THINKING ABOUT (paused at lunch, 12 Sep):

  Do landlords ever get a written channel that is NOT a case reply?

  Today they do not. Contact Us is behind login and landlords have no
  account, so a landlord with a general question — why am I receiving
  these, or a data-protection request — has only the address published
  on /landlords. That makes admin@ the single front door for every
  landlord enquiry that is not a case reply, which raises its
  importance well above tidiness.

  This decides whether admin@ is a STOPGAP or the PERMANENT front
  door, and whether the threaded Contact Us is tenant-only forever.
  If landlords are ever to be inside a thread they need either an
  account or the token-in-the-reply-address mechanism already built
  for case replies.

CONTACT US, details not settled:
  - May a customer post again without waiting for an answer? Free
    posting is more natural and makes the record honest; reply-only is
    tidier but stops a customer adding "it happened again".
  - What does the notification email contain? A link and a subject
    keeps the thread as the real record; including the message text
    makes email the conversation and the thread a copy.
  - What happens when someone hits reply in Outlook anyway, because
    they will? Either point the reply address at the forwarded admin
    address, or say in the email that replies are not read. Silently
    discarding them is the thing being fixed, so it cannot be the
    answer.

ADMIN MAIL, not settled:
  - Should Outlook SEND AS admin@ through Mailgun's sending
    credentials, so Charlie's private address stays out of the
    headers? Forwarding alone means a reply from Outlook exposes the
    private address to landlords and data subjects. Same exposure
    argument as #42, applied to email instead of post.
  - The apex mail posture (#33) is now DEFERRED, not decided. Using
    the mg. address means no apex decision is needed today. #33 still
    contemplates a null MX so that apex mail fails cleanly; that
    remains open and should be taken together with #33's SPF
    tightening.
